Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

Jimothy quickly flies his ship to the top of the elevated island. The wall of water gets taller and larger as it comes racing towards him. The tidal wave crashes against the island, but he is elevated and far enough to just nearly miss it. After being stranded there for what felt like days, he spots a ship just like his in the night sky. Another Jimnetian! He does everything to get its attention, and it finally notices him. The Jimnetian lands, and Jimothy climbs aboard the ship. The pilot brings them to the pack of Jimnetians travelling together, and Jimothy is relieved. They are heading back to Jimnet, so the evacuation must be over already. He reunites with his family, and they make their way back to Jimnet, safe and sound.


Jimothy has been reunited!
