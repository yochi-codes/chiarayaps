The Interview

Mr. Smith laughs, nods slowly.

"You have ambition, I like that. What if I told you I plan to do exactly that?"

He stands up.

"Before I share with you my plan, there is something I must tell you first. The truth is, I know I appear as a normal man, but in reality..."

You watch as he starts... morphing. What's happening in front of you is disgusting, but you can't look away. His arms tuck into his body, his abodomen starts growing appendages. He looks like- like a... cockroach? You wait for an answer.

"...I am a cockroach, but I was genetically modified, gifted with special abilities. However, I'm now much more advanced than my peers, my family. I'm estranged by them, and hated by humans. They always fear something stronger than them. My purpose is to make all cockroaches more advanced than humans. If they want something to be scared of, we'll give them something to be scared of. However, I need a human partner. I simply do not have as much freedom and access to resources as you."

He explains his plan, to evolve cockroaches, making them smarter, stronger than any other species. His offers you the position of his partner, and you agree.
