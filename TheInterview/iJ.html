The Interview

Mr. Smith laughs, nods slowly.

"You're very ambitious. I like that. I really do. You'll be perfect for this position."

He stands up.

"Before I share with you my plan, there is something I must tell you first. The truth is, I know I appear as a normal man, but in reality..."

You watch as he starts... morphing. What's happening in front of you is disgusting, but you can't look away. His arms tuck into his body, his abodomen starts growing appendages. He looks like- like a... cockroach? You wait for an answer.

"...I am a cockroach. As a species, we have always been stepped on and trampled by humans. All they want is domination. And for thousands of years, they got it. Now, it's our turn. A revolution is underway."

He explains his scheme for a cockroach revolution, and eventually, domination. You agree to be part of the cause, though you're not sure you had much of a choice.
