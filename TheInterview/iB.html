The Interview

Mr. Smith smiles.

"I'm glad you think that way."

He stands up.

"The truth is, I know I appear as a normal man, but in reality,"

You watch as he starts... morphing. What's happening in front of you is disgusting, but you can't look away. His arms tuck into his body, his abodomen starts growing appendages. He looks like- like a... cockroach? You wait for an answer.

"I- I know this looks strange. But, I founded this company to reach a lifelong dream of mine: for humans to tolerate cockroaches like me, and to end the extermination of my kind. However, I can't acheive this goal by myself. I need a human, like you, to advocate for my species. So, the position I was hiring for was a partner - a diplomat. You are the best person I was hoping to find. Therefore, I hope you accept his offer."

You agree to work with him. Together, you form a movement for cockroach rights.
