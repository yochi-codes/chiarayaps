The Interview

Mr. Smith smiles.

"I agree," he says, "I've been trying to make that happen for a long time."

He stands up, and sighs.

"Before I can explain how, there is something I must show you. The truth is, I know I appear as a normal man, but in reality..."

You watch as he starts... morphing. What's happening in front of you is disgusting, but you can't look away. His arms tuck into his body, his abodomen starts growing appendages. He looks like- like a... cockroach? You wait for an answer.

"...I am a cockroach. For millenia, humans and cockroachs have been at odds. Humans have always hated us. However, I dream of a world where cockroaches and humans co-exist. My first step to making that dream a reality is proving that humans and cockroaches can work together, share the same goals. That's why I need you, someone who wants to make the world a better place. We can work together as partners, our small alliance will be the first step to peace."

You agree to join him, to make a new idealistic world together. You don't know if it's possible, but you will try.
