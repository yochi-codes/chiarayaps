The Interview

"Hmm... I like the way you think." he says.

He seems to ponder on your answer for a few seconds. You wonder if he's heard that before from previous interviewees.

And if he has, you wonder if they were hired.

"Let's change gears a little bit. Let's say there is a species that is extremely resilient. Anything humans throw at them, they survive it. Would you consider that a problem?"

Choose a response:

"No, that sounds like an advantage. Especially if we could study them."

"Yes, anything that survives that easily is probably dangerous. If they choose to attack, we wouldn't have a defense."
