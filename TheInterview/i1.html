The Interview

"Interesting," he says.

You can't tell is he's pleased or not, but he's intrigued. He writes something down in his notebook, and you try to take a peak at what's on it. His handwriting is illegible though - it doesn't even look like a language you recognize.

"Suppose improving the lives of one group makes thing somewhat worse for another. Would you still do it?"

Choose a response:

"No, it's not worth it. Harming someone for the benefit of someone else isn't right."

"Definitely, for the greater good. You can't make everyone happy, so sometimes you have to make sacrifices."
