The Interview

You walk into the office of Mr. Smith. You can tell he is a manager in Smith Research Labs. The company specializes in something to do with cockroaches, but you don't know exactly what. He greets you as you take a seat in front of his desk, and gives you a firm handshake. He asks for your name, despite having your resume ready in his hands. You don't really know what you've applied for exactly, but you're still here. He looks you up and down; then he gets straight to business.

"My name is John Smith, I'm the founder and owner of Smith Research Labs. I'm going to ask you some questions, but I'm not very interested in experience or hard skills. The position we're hiring for requires a... different skillset. So, we'll start with something easy: if there was one thing you change about the world, what would it be?"

Choose a response:

"I would make people's live better."

"I would make the world more efficient."

"I would end all conflict."
