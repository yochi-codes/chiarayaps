The Interview

Mr. Smith laughs, nods slowly.

"I like that. What if I told you I plan to do exactly that?"

He stands up.

"Before I share with you my plan, there is something I must tell you first. The truth is, I know I appear as a normal man, but in reality..."

You watch as he starts... morphing. What's happening in front of you is disgusting, but you can't look away. His arms tuck into his body, his abodomen starts growing appendages. He looks like- like a... cockroach? You wait for an answer.

"...I am a cockroach, but I was genetically modified, gifted with special abilities. Cockroaches and humans have never acheived peace. Mainly because the humans detest my kind, but we didn't have the cognitive ability to defend ourselves. Until a few years ago, when I was blessed with the chance to speak up. However, the humans still don't listen. That is why I need someone like you, a human negotiator, working to convince society that an alliance can be acheived."

He explains his plan to form a political alliance between both species, and that forming your own alliance as partners is proof that it can happen. You agree to be his partner.
