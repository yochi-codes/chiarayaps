Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

Jimothy sprints as fast as he can towards his ship. As he can hear the volcano erupt, he starts his engines, using the last bit of fuel he has. Ash and lava come raining down, right as his ship rises towards the sky in an attempt for a last-minute getaway into outer space. However, the engines fail; Jimothy has run out of fuel! His ship crashes down to the surface, now covered in molten lava. Thankfully, the Jimnetians have engineered their ship to withstand much higher temperatures, but after the lava cools, Jimothy has no way to escape the rock. Jimothy is lost there forever.
