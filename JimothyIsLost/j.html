Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

After his home planet, Jimnet, had to be evacuated, Jimothy was seperated from his family, friends, and everyone else. Jimothy is lost in outer space. With only a small spaceship and a couple days in space, Jimothy is running out of nearly everything he needs to survive. He was supposed to meet with the rest of Jimnet's inhabitants at a nearby planet, but Jimothy forgot which planet it was. Before him are three planets. His Jimtech Celestial Object Indentifier tells him that the red planet is made of Jimnite, which is what the Jimnetians use as a fuel source. The blue planet is covered in water, which Jimothy needs to survive. The green planet is teeming with life and covered in jungle. Jimothy might be able to find food there, but there's also a high chance of many other creatures looking to make him their lunch.

Which planet should Jimothy attempt to land on?
