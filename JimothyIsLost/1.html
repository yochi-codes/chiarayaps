Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

With the last of his fuel, Jimothy makes his way to the red planet. The first thing he notices is the abundant amount of Jimnite deposits. After he lands on solid ground, Jimothy grabs a few empty Jimnite containers and sets up his portable Jimnite Extraction Purifier. Then, Jimothy decides to wait no longer and search for any other Jimnetians who may have landed here. However, in less than an hour, the ground starts to shake, and Jimothy hears a loud, deafening noise. The air temperature rises quickly, and Jimothy realizes a nearby volcano is about to erupt. Jimothy only has a few minutes to escape. He considers his options. He might not have enough time to reach now-ready fuel and then his ship, but there is a possibility. He also spots a nearby safe area that he could possibly get to in time, but no doubt the ship and fuel would be lost.

Where should Jimothy go?
