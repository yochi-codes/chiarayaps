Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

Jimothy sprints as fast as he can towards the fuel containers he prepared. As he can hear the volcano erupt, he grasps the handles of two containers and redirects towards his ship. Ash and lava come raining down, right as he jumps into his ship and makes a last-minute getaway into outer space. Once he leaves the atmosphere, he spots a ship just like his in the distance. Another Jimnetian! He has enough fuel to meet up with them, and finds the pack of Jimnetians travelling together. They are heading back to Jimnet! The evacuation must be over already! He reunites with his family, and they make their way back to Jimnet, safe and sound.


Jimothy has been reunited!
