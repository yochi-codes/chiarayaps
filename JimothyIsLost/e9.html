Jimothy Is Lost (Remastered)

Jimothy hides behind a rock, but the monster is too smart to miss it. The monster charges towards Jimothy. Paralyzed with fear, Jimothy can't move a muscle. With one hand, the monster grasps Jimothy's small body. The monster holds Jimothy in front of its face, and then gives him a saliva-spraying roarrr. It takes Jimothy to its cave, and wraps him in a cocoon-like substance. He ties Jimothy to the ceiling of the cave, so he is hanging upside down. When the cave is illuminated enough, Jimothy sees hundreds of Jimnetians hanging with him. It seems everyone from his planet has met the same fate.
