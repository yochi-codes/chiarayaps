My Favorite Marvel Characters

Recently, I have a had very sudden but strong burst of interest in the MCU. My goal is to watch all the movies in order of release, but when I am not watching the movies my obsessive personality prevents me from doing or thinking about anything else. I'm around half way through. So without further ado, here are my favorite characters from a baby fan perspective.

Spider-Man

Specifically Tom Holland's Spider-Man.

I've always like Spiderman, especially since it was the one Marvel movie series that I would consistently watch. I like how it's more laid back than all the other Marvel movies. I also like Peter's personality, I think he's a sweetheart.

Yelena Belova/Black Widow

My ultimate cutie patootie.

I love Yelena's character so much, especially how Florence Pugh portrays her. I love how she's funny, but kinda in a deadpan way, and I love how she wears her heart on her sleeve. I also really like how she's still fun, even with a tragic parts, but also has a lot of emotion. I'm really happy that Marvel is moving in a direction with her as a main character, and I think this is the best switch/replacement of a character ever.

Loki

Haven't watched the show yet.

I can't really explain why I like him, but I think he's not a bad character, per se. He's just very complicated, and I think that's what I like. But other than that, I can't say much.

So there you have it! My three favorite Marvel characters. But hopefully I will make some new ones as I make my way through the movies!
